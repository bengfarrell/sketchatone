## How the strings work

Imagine strings running across your graphics tablet. When your stylus crosses one, it gets plucked. Sketchatone sends that note to your synth or DAW, where it becomes sound.

Move slowly to pick out individual notes. Sweep faster and the notes come together as a single strum. You're not limited to six strings, either: you can choose how many to use.

The spacing and number of strings are yours to adjust. When you're ready to change the feel, the [strumming settings](/about/strumming/) explain how.

<h2 id="make-it-expressive">Pressure, tilt, and position</h2>

Moving across the strings chooses when to play a note. The way you hold and press the pen can change how that note sounds.

**Pressure can control loudness.** Press lightly for softer notes, or harder for louder ones. This uses MIDI note velocity, so the response depends on your synth patch. Read more about [pressure and note velocity](/about/note-velocity/).

**Position can control pitch bend.** Moving up or down the tablet can bend the notes you're playing. See the [pitch bend settings](/about/pitch-bend/).

**Tilt can control note length.** Changing the angle of your pen can make notes shorter or let them ring longer. Read about [note length and sustain](/about/note-duration/).

## Change what the pen controls

These are examples, not fixed assignments. Pressure, tilt, and vertical position can be mapped to different controls. What they do depends on the preset you're using.

Try changing one thing at a time so you can hear what it does. You can change these assignments in the [dashboard's parameter mappings](/about/dashboard/).
